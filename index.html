FAQ

Is the AI really running on my computer?

Yes. AutoAgent uses a free open-source model (Qwen3) through Ollama on your own PC. Our servers only handle your login, subscription and the phone remote connection.

What PC do I need?

Windows 10 or 11. For best results an NVIDIA GPU with at least 6 GB VRAM and 16 GB RAM. Older PCs work with a smaller model, but slower and less accurate.

How does the phone remote work?

Sign in on your phone with the same account. Your PC appears automatically. Tasks you send run on your PC and results appear on your phone. Your PC must be on with AutoAgent running.

What is the founder price?

The first 1000 Pro subscribers pay $19.99/month — and keep that price for good, even if they cancel and come back later.

What is AutoAgent mode?

An add-on for Pro users (+$5/month). Instead of answering one task, the agent plans a whole project: it creates its own task list, completes each task, reviews its progress and writes a full report. You need an active Pro plan to buy and use it.

Can I cancel?

Yes, anytime from the link in your receipt email. Pro stays active until the end of the paid period.

Is the AI always right?

No AI is. AutoAgent shows every step and its sources so you can check results before relying on them.
